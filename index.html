01 — About

A builder’s mindset, on and off the course.

I’m a junior at Claremont McKenna College studying Economics (GPA 3.8), where I turn classroom theory into hands-on quantitative work — from valuing companies across Harvard Business School cases to pricing equity derivatives with Black-Scholes-Merton models and hedging portfolios using the Greeks.

Off the page, I compete for the Claremont-Mudd-Scripps Men’s Golf Team, which captured the NCAA Division III National Championship. Golf taught me what finance demands: discipline under pressure, precise decision-making, and the patience to grind through complex problems one shot — or one model — at a time.

Whether I’m stress-testing a valuation or rallying my team back from a 13-stroke deficit, I bring the same things: preparation, composure, and a genuine drive to win.
